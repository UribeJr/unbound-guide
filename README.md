# Pokémon Unbound · Complete Explorer's Guide

Open `index.html` directly (file://). There's no build step and no network needed: fonts are inlined and every image is local (`assets/` holds the hand-made Part 1 pages, `img/` holds Parts 2–40 and the gyms).

**What's inside:** 925 pages covering all 40 parts of https://unboundwiki.com/walkthrough/ (Part 40 is the post-game "Ruins of Void & Saving Mom"), 8 gym-leader spreads with Vanilla / Difficult / Expert / Insane teams, a contents page and an end/credits page.

- **Desktop (≥820px):** two-page spread with 3D page turns.
  - Turn pages by clicking a page corner, using NEXT/PREV, or pressing ←/→ (Home/End also work).
  - 10 colored chapter tabs sit on the page edges. Tabs for chapters you've passed move to the left side.
  - 8 badge tabs under the book open each gym leader's spread.
  - Contents entries are clickable.
  - Only about 6 leaves are in the DOM at once. Long jumps teleport the book and then riffle the last 2 leaves.
- **Mobile:** single page with a page-turn animation and swipe. Chapter and gym chips sit at the top. Only the current page ±1 is rendered.
- **PokéGear** (button, or press G): WILD / ITEMS / TRAINERS / BADGES for the location on the open page (210 sections, also in a grouped dropdown).
  - It follows the book unless you switch that off.
  - Progress is saved in localStorage under `unbound-guide-v1`: caught, items, trainers, badges, gym difficulty and page.
  - The ball buttons on the wild cards, the item cards and the WIN? buttons on trainer cards share this state.
- **Images:** WebP and lazy-loaded. Maps are resized for the page, and the full-size versions in `img/full/` open in the lightbox.

Structure:
- `js/pages.js`: the hand-made Part 1 pages.
- `js/render.js`: page templates for generated pages.
- `data/book.js`: pagination, chapters, gyms and PokéGear locations.
- `data/pN.js`: per-part content.
- `js/app.js`: book engine.
- `data/config.js`: `LITE` flag.

The pages were generated by the build scripts in /workspace/unbound-build. Those scripts parse the wiki HTML, convert images, measure every block in a headless browser and paginate at build time.

## Data rules / gaps
- Only what unboundwiki.com prints is shown. Anything missing is marked **TBD**.
- Wild encounter rates and levels: the walkthrough's wild tables list species only, so they show "Rate / Lv. TBD".
- Prize money for trainers in Parts 2–40 isn't in the walkthrough: TBD.
- Gym teams come from each /gyms/ page. A note appears on a spread when the walkthrough disagrees with the gym page (Tessy's Seismitoad: Lv.55 vs Lv.56).

## v3 · "Trainer's Journal" theme (Oct 2026)
- Look: kraft/cream dotted paper with grain, a spiral binding, washi-taped polaroid screenshots, photo-corner maps, sticky-note tips, index-card warnings, and coloured sticky chapter tabs. Red-pen and pencil annotations draw themselves in when a page appears (GSAP DrawSVG).
- Pokémon sprites show as pencil sketches until you mark them caught. Click the sprite or the ball button and a die-cut sticker slaps on. It is saved in localStorage.
- Each gym page has a leather badge case. "Stamp it in!" slams a rubber-stamp ink ring (an SVG turbulence filter) and fills the badge.
- Opening: a Three.js desk scene (`js/intro.js`, lazy-loaded `vendor/three.min.js`). Click or tap the journal to open it. **Skip intro** is remembered; you can turn it back on or replay it in PokéGear ⚙. Reduced motion or no WebGL falls back to a flat 2D cover. On mobile the scene uses pixel ratio 1, no shadows and no antialiasing.
- Poké Ball toggles (`.pbt`): Night mode (desk-lamp lighting), Follow book page, Desk intro.
- Fonts (OFL, inlined in `css/fonts-journal.css`): Permanent Marker (display), Caveat (handwriting), plus the existing Barlow (body/data) and Press Start 2P (small accents).
- Vendored libraries: `vendor/gsap.min.js` + `DrawSVGPlugin.min.js` (GSAP standard license, free), `vendor/three.min.js` r158 (MIT). Licenses are in `vendor/`.
- Theme layer: `css/journal.css` (loaded after `guide.css`); badge pixel art: `js/badges.js`.
